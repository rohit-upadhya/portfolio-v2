I'm building an RTS set in the Indian subcontinent. It's called Prachin Kaal, it's a homage to Age of Empires, and this is the first note on it.

## where it started

*The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Ring* was the first RTS game that I ever played. The fact that resource management was something that needed taking care of, managing units and resources, sparked something in me that has never died.

## the age of empires

What really got me hooked was Age of Empires III. While AoE 2 is the more popular version of AoE, I have never played AoE 2. AoE 3, however, I have spent countless hours on — not only in the campaign mode, but in skirmishes with the AI.

In college, we played the LAN-based multiplayer, and would legit spend days playing games. A single game would take at least 4 hours to complete.

## student of history

While I always liked history, Age of Empires sparked an even deeper interest in it. I dove deep into European history especially. I read extensively about the various civilizations in AoE 3, and about each civ's advantages and why those advantages exist.

What I really wanted to see, however, was an RTS game based in the Indian subcontinent. We have incredible history here, but there wasn't any game that would scratch that itch. So I am attempting to build one myself.

## what it is

Currently Prachin Kaal only has 4 civs and one campaign mode. The civs are the Gorkha Kingdom, the Company, the Ahom Kingdom, and the Mughal Empire. There are 10 maps — Himalaya, Terai, Meghalaya, Kerala, Sundarbans, Thar, Hindu Kush, Ganga, Andaman, Ceylon — each with their own quirks.

The campaign is about a Gorkha soldier telling his grandson the story of fighting the British, while the grandson is himself about to join the British army as a Gorkha.

The game also has multiple realistic animals, some of them carnivores that actively attack your units.

## how it works

The game is built in Godot, with unit assets generated using AI and sounds created with ElevenLabs.

Since this is an RTS, the game plays without turns. There is a treaty mode, which lets players concentrate on building a town and collecting resources before committing to a decent attack.

I am also building two separate modes, Multiplayer and Play Online. I will keep servers on my end, but players can also create servers themselves.

## what's next

Next, we expand to more maps and more civs.
